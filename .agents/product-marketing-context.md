# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-04-09*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:**
Production AI systems for companies that have outgrown ChatGPT.

**What it does:**
Tahona helps companies move from scattered AI experiments to production AI systems that actually run inside real operations. The work combines strategy, context and data foundation, AI agents and automations, and training so clients can deploy useful systems without rebuilding their whole stack.

**Product category:**
Hybrid: AI agency, AI consultancy, and production AI partner

**Product type:**
B2B service business

**Business model:**
Service-led. Four service lines sold standalone or combined: AI Strategy & Consulting, Context Management & Data Architecture, AI Agents & Automations, Training & Enablement.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:**
SMBs, typically under 50–100 employees, with enough operational complexity for AI to create meaningful leverage.

**Decision-makers:**
Founders, owners, managing directors, and business/unit directors.

**Primary use case:**
A company has already experimented with AI tools or lightweight automation, but now needs reliable, contextual, production-grade systems that integrate with day-to-day operations.

**Jobs to be done:**
- Turn AI experimentation into production systems
- Capture company knowledge so AI can use it reliably
- Automate repetitive, knowledge-heavy operational work
- Decide where AI creates real value before overinvesting

**Use cases:**
- Internal knowledge assistance
- Document processing and classification
- Data extraction and validation
- Customer support workflows
- Sales or lead qualification
- Process monitoring and alerting
- Cross-system workflow automation

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:**
Most companies can try AI now, but very few can make it work reliably in production.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Generic consultancies stay high-level and theoretical
- Freelancers may build point fixes without strategic depth
- AI hype agencies sell impressive demos that break in real use
- Tool vendors force one-size-fits-all workflows
- Internal prompt usage and fragile automations do not scale across teams

**What it costs them:**
Manual work, process bottlenecks, inconsistent decisions, lost knowledge, duplicated effort, delayed execution, and poor AI adoption.

**Emotional tension:**
Frustration that AI "kind of works" but not where it matters. Anxiety about wasting budget, choosing the wrong use case, or building on top of messy data and broken processes.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:**
AI agencies and AI consultancies — fall short when they stay strategic without building, or build without embedding in real operations.

**Secondary:**
Freelancers, automation shops, no-code consultants, generic software consultancies — fall short when they solve isolated tasks but not the operational system around them.

**Indirect:**
Internal teams using ChatGPT, ad hoc prompts, simple n8n flows, spreadsheets, manual coordination, or legacy processes — fall short because they remain fragile, person-dependent, and hard to scale.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Production-first approach
- Strong emphasis on context management
- Technology partner mindset, not vendor mindset
- Combines strategy and implementation
- Builds around the client’s real operations and existing stack
- Operates in the gap between rigid SaaS and heavyweight consultancies

**How we do it differently:**
Tahona starts with the business problem, learns the client’s domain, structures the context and data layer, and builds AI systems that integrate into daily operations rather than living as disconnected experiments.

**Why that’s better:**
It produces systems that are actually usable, maintainable, and valuable in production, not just impressive in demos.

**Why customers choose us:**
Because Tahona is small enough to move fast, senior enough to think strategically, and technical enough to build custom systems that larger firms make too expensive and SaaS cannot solve cleanly.

**Pull:**

**Habit:**

**Anxiety:**
Fear of wasting money on hype, disrupting operations, choosing the wrong tools, or building something the team cannot maintain.

## Customer Language
**How they describe the problem:**
- "We know we need AI, but we don’t know where to start."
- "We’ve tried prompts and small automations, but it doesn’t scale."
- "Our knowledge is spread across docs, systems, and people."
- "We need this to work in the real operation, not in a demo."

**How they describe us:**
- "They make AI actually work in production."
- "They understand the business problem, not just the tooling."
- "They build around our processes instead of forcing us into a product."

**Words to use:**
production, operations, context, systems, automation, integration, traceability, real workflows, useful, reliable, measurable

**Words to avoid:**
